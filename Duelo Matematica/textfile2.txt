The Controllers (The "Brains")
Controllers listen for clicks, validate input, and tell the rest of the app what to do.


The Models (The "Rules & Data")
Models store data and handle the rules (like math, database logic, and session memory). They know NOTHING about buttons or screens.



The Views (The "Faces")
Views build the UI. They don't know what the buttons do; they just hand the buttons to the Controller.
